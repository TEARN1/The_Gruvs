## ⚠️ Read first — the SMART way to handle "men, women, kids"
The naive version ("show men X, women Y") is a **trap** — it's stereotyping, it's
legally risky (discrimination), and it's reductive. The masterpiece way:
- **Personalize by what people *self-declare and do*** (interests, scenes they
  Touch Down at, who they Crew with) — **never by assuming from gender.**
- **Women: safety is the lever, not different content.** Women decide if an app
  is safe to use — so the "women's experience" = the strongest safety in the
  category (verified-only, control who sees you, no cold DMs, safe-ride-home,
  panic, women-only event option for hosts). Nail that and women bring women.
- **Kids/teens: gate, don't target.** Nightlife is 18+. Minors get an
  all-ages-only mode (sports, hackathons, festivals, community — no alcohol/
  adult, no stranger DMs, no Crossed Paths) — or the app is simply 18+ at launch.
- **Age-bands differ by *scene*, not assumption** — a 19-yr-old and a 40-yr-old
  pick different Gruvs; let their own taste + the age gate decide, not a guess.
- Principle: **welcomes, never excludes; safe + relevant for everyone; driven by
  self-declared identity + the age gate + safety-by-need.**
