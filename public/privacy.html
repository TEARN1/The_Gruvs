The Gruvs ("we", "us") helps you discover events, connect with other vibers, and build your talent profile. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account — email, username, display name, and profile details you provide (bio, photos, interests).
  • Location — with your permission, to show nearby events and vibers. You can disable location in your device settings or use Ghost mode in-app.
  • Activity — events you RSVP to, vibes, reactions, reels, check-ins, follows, and content you post.
  • Device — push notification token (if enabled) and basic technical data for reliability.

How we use it

  • Provide and personalise the app (recommendations, nearby discovery, your talent/career profile).
  • Notifications you opt into (event reminders, messages, mentions).
  • Safety, fraud prevention, and to enforce our Terms.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by our infrastructure providers (e.g. Supabase for database/auth, hosting providers) solely to run the service. Public profile fields, posts, and event participation may be visible to other users by design.

Your choices & rights

  • Edit or hide profile details, and control discoverability and online status in-app.
  • Disable location and push notifications at any time.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Settings → Security; deletion is permanent and erases your data. See how to delete your account.
  • Request access to or correction of your data by contacting us.

Data retention & security

We keep data while your account is active and remove it on deletion (subject to legal obligations). Access is protected by authentication and database row-level security.

Children

The Gruvs is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).
