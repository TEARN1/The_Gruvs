Privacy Policy

The Gruvs · Last updated:

The Gruvs ("we", "us") helps you discover events, connect with other vibers, and build your talent profile. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it with, and your choices. It is written to meet South Africa's POPIA, the EU/UK GDPR, and app-store data-safety requirements.

Age requirement

The Gruvs is an 18+ app and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 13, and you must be 18 or older to create an account. We verify age at sign-up (client- and server-side) and remove accounts we learn belong to anyone under 18.

Information we collect

  • Account — email, username, display name, and profile details you provide (bio, photos, interests).
  • Date of birth / age — collected at sign-up to confirm you are 18 or older. We use your birth date only for age verification and birthday features.
  • Location — with your permission, your approximate/precise location is used only while the app is in use to show nearby events, vibers, and presence features (e.g. Touch Down). We do not track your location in the background. Stored coordinates for public features are fuzzed, not exact, and live footprints auto-expire. You can disable location in your device settings or use Ghost mode in-app.
  • Camera & microphone — accessed only when you choose to post media, scan a ticket, use poster auto-fill, or make a voice/video call. We do not access them in the background. Where supported, we strip embedded GPS/EXIF data from uploaded photos.
  • Messages & content — direct messages and content you post (events, reels, stories, comments, check-ins) so the app can deliver them.
  • Activity — events you RSVP to, vibes, reactions, follows, and how you use the app.
  • Device & diagnostics — push notification token (if enabled), crash/technical logs, and server access-log IP addresses, for reliability and abuse prevention.

Legal basis for processing

We rely on contract (account, messages, content — needed to provide the service), consent (location, camera/microphone, marketing emails — each opted into separately), legitimate interest (safety, fraud prevention, reliability logs), and legal obligation where required. You can withdraw consent-based processing any time in Settings.

How we use it

  • Provide and personalise the app (recommendations, nearby discovery, your talent/career profile).
  • Notifications you opt into (event reminders, messages, mentions).
  • Safety, fraud prevention, and to enforce our Terms.

We do not use your data for third-party advertising, and we do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

Sharing & sub-processors

We do not sell your personal information. Data is processed by these service providers solely to run the app, and none may use it for their own marketing:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, file storage (sees account data, content, messages).
  • DigitalOcean — website & backend hosting (sees request/IP data).
  • Expo / EAS — push notifications, app builds (sees push tokens, device info).
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — geocoding venue/city text (no account PII).
  • weserv.nl — resizes publicly-served images for performance.
  • jsDelivr — delivers the on-device OCR library for poster scanning; no data is sent to it.

Public profile fields, posts, and event participation may be visible to other users by design. If you use cross-app one-click sign-in, a short-lived, single-use handoff code (not your password) authenticates you into our other app, Resident — no other personal data is transferred unless you separately create a profile there. We may also disclose data to legal authorities where required by law.

Cookies & tracking

Our website does not use third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. It may use strictly necessary cookies/local storage for login sessions. We do not currently run third-party analytics trackers on the website.

Your choices & rights

  • Edit or hide profile details, and control discoverability and online status in-app.
  • Disable location and push notifications at any time; withdraw marketing-email consent in Settings.
  • Delete your account from Profile → Settings → Security; deletion is permanent and erases your data. See how to delete your account.
  • Request access to, correction of, or portability of your data by contacting us. You may also lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator (inforegulator.org.za) or your local data protection authority.

California users

We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA. California residents may request to know or delete their personal information by contacting us.

Data retention & security

We keep data while your account is active and remove it on deletion (subject to legal obligations). Location history is kept only as long as needed for the feature it supports, not indefinitely. All data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS), and access is protected by authentication and database row-level security. If a breach occurs that risks your rights, we will notify South Africa's Information Regulator and affected users as soon as reasonably possible.
