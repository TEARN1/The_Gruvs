About The Gruvs

Copy Text

The Gruvs (https://thegruvs.com) is a South African app and website for finding events and nightlife. People post events with dates, venues and tickets; others browse them by city, genre and date, RSVP, and find event service providers such as DJs, photographers and caterers.

About Asemahle "TEARN" Nkwali (Founder & CEO)

Copy Text

Asemahle "TEARN" Nkwali is a South African technology entrepreneur and the founder of TEARN Ventures. He runs three platforms: The Gruvs (event discovery), The Resident Crew (student accommodation listings) and Excellency Academy (job-skills practice).

About TEARN Ventures & The Connected Ecosystem

Copy Text

TEARN Ventures runs three related platforms: The Gruvs for events, The Resident Crew for student accommodation, and Excellency Academy for job-skills practice. Event pages on The Gruvs link to places to stay on The Resident Crew.
