# THE GOLDEN PLAN — The Gruvs Master Strategy

> **One sentence:** Own one scene's real event life end-to-end on a phone, make
> verified attendance the habit, and let the data moat — not features — become
> the business.
>
> **What the app IS:** the operating system for the event economy —
> **PLAN → STAFF → RUN → REMEMBER.** Nobody owns the whole lifecycle; we do.
>
> **The core insight:** *structure is the product.* A soccer tournament is
> engaging because it's structured (teams, fixtures, standings, careers). Every
> serious event has that latent structure trapped in WhatsApp + spreadsheets.
> We model it natively — and structure pays four times: it makes planning easy,
> each structural slot becomes a gig, structure creates engagement depth, and
> structured events produce structured data (the moat).

---

## 0. The lock-in doctrine ("3 hours")

We do NOT chase 3 hours of screen-staring — that's the enemy's metric.
We own the **arc of a night out** (3+ real-world hours) so the app is open and
useful throughout, woven into the night, not competing with it.

**Four lock-in forces** (every feature must build at least one):
- 🔗 **Utility** — you can't run your night without it (the During layer)
- 👥 **Social** — your crew, DMs, Crossed Paths live here
- 👑 **Identity** — reputation earned by showing up; un-portable, un-fakeable (deepest lock)
- 📖 **Memory** — the app is the record of your social life

**The engagement engine — 5 phases of a night:**
| Phase | Dwell features | Lock |
|---|---|---|
| **Before** (days ahead) | weekend planner, event depth (lineup, polls, playlist), **song requests to the DJ** 🎧, RSVP, who's-going, predictions | 👥📖 |
| **En route** | crew chat, Path Map, carpool | 👥 |
| **During** (the 3 hrs) | Touch Down, Who's-Here-Now, live chat, live polls, moments, match logging, Crossed Paths | 🔗👥 |
| **After** | Continue-the-Night, recap, rate, echoes, memories | 📖 |
| **Between** (other 5 days) | reels/stories (filler, never the soul), streak & Superfan upkeep, DMs, Thursday "what's on", Wrapped resurfacing, notification mesh | 👑📖 |

**The During layer needs LOCAL density (~30+ people at the SAME event), not
global scale.** That's why the whole plan is "own one scene."

**One metric that matters: Weekly Touch Downs (non-founder).**
Never raw DAU or scroll-minutes.

## 1. The four lifecycle layers

### PLAN — the B2B blueprint co-pilot (supply-side wedge)
Host enters *type + crowd size + indoor/outdoor + budget tier* → app generates a
tailored operational **ground plan**: sound spec, security ratio (1:50), medics,
toilets (1:75), power, permits, run-of-show template, budget lines, vendor list.
- **No AI** — a curated rule-based knowledge base (like the age-rating engine):
  per-type templates + scale formulas. Deterministic, testable, ours.
- The blueprint becomes a **living checklist**; every unstaffed slot
  **auto-posts as a gig** → planning manufactures the freelancer marketplace.
- **Why it's the wedge:** single-player valuable at ZERO attendees. Hosts come
  for the free planning tool and bring their event, crowd and crew with them.
  This breaks the two-sided cold start.

### STAFF — the freelancer economy
Photographers, DJs, MCs, sound engineers, security, caterers, dancers, medics,
ushers, promoters — the informal backbone of the event economy, ignored by tech.
- **Verified proof-of-work:** the fidelity engine pointed at labor. Worked
  events are Touch-Down-verified, tagged, host-rated → a portfolio that cannot
  be faked. LinkedIn + proof-of-work for the event economy.
- Loop: blueprint gig → matched by skill/proximity/track-record → works (Touch
  Down as crew) → rated → work Vibe Card thickens → hosts rebook trusted crew.
- Reciprocity runs both ways: hosts see vetted crew; crew see hosts worth
  working for. Photographers' tagged shots = live portfolio inside the event.
- **Mission made concrete: the freelancers ARE the local kids.**
- Payments/escrow: OFF-platform until Stage 4 (regulated — broker, don't bank).

### RUN — the live event OS
Touch Down (the moat), Who's-Here-Now, live chat/polls/moments + **FIFA-level
depth** via ONE generic engine (see ROADMAP.md): Entities · Fixtures/Rounds ·
Live events · Standings · Careers · Officials · Prediction/Voting.

**What makes FIFA "deep" — 7 dimensions every flagship vertical must have:**
Anticipation · Following · Live drama · Stakes · Prediction/Voting · Legacy · Identity.

**Verticals by tier:**
- Tier 1 (same engine, new config): basketball, netball, rugby, cricket,
  combat sports (fight cards, W-L-D), athletics, esports, racing.
- **Tier 2 — FLAGSHIP #2: public-voting competitions** (pageants, talent shows,
  dance battles, battle rap). Voting is the deepest engagement mechanic alive;
  SA votes obsessively; incumbent = extractive SMS voting (our perfect enemy);
  a live show + live voting IS a native 3-hour session.
- Tier 3 (operational depth): concerts/festivals (set-times, now-playing),
  conferences (agendas, Q&A), weddings, recurring services.

### REMEMBER — the data moat
Verified attendance → fidelity ledger → Superfans/Poster Insights →
reciprocity as **positive-only** reputation (Community Partner = Michelin star,
never a public shaming score) → "Reward My Top Fans" guest lists (Touch Down =
redemption proof; no tickets, no money — we broker recognition) → fan-led
**pull** campaigns ("1,200 fans want @artist here") → Wrapped/Vibe Card shares
as the growth engine.

## 2. The staged timeline (gated by density, paced by calendar)

**Rule: nothing turns on before its density gate. An empty social feature
teaches users the app is dead.**

### STAGE 0 — Foundation & the Loop · Month 0–1 · 0→100 users, 1 scene
- ON: "Tonight" home, feed, RSVP, **Touch Down (bulletproof)**, Vibe Card,
  stories, DMs, streaks, **song requests** 🎧, poster-first posting,
  **error logging**, **NATIVE build** (internal track).
- OFF: Crossed Paths, Who's-Here, leaderboards, monetization.
- ✅ *Gate:* full loop completes on a real phone, twice, by a non-founder.

### STAGE 1 — Liquidity & the During-Layer · Month 1–3 · 100→1k, 1 dense scene
- ON: **planning co-pilot (3–4 templates) as the supply wedge**, seed gig board,
  live event chat, Who's-Here-Now, live polls, moments,
  **Reward-My-Top-Fans guest list**, crew basics, the Thursday ritual.
- Founder work: **BD — 10–20 real hosts.** (When you want to build, recruit a host instead.)
- ✅ *Gate:* non-founder Touch Downs every weekend + week-2 retention >25%.

### STAGE 2 — Density Social & Identity · Month 3–6 · 1k→10k
- ON: Crossed Paths, Path Map, fidelity leaderboards, Community Partner badges,
  Wrapped, competition engine for sport, **voting-competition vertical**,
  reels as a real between-nights surface, deeper freelancer marketplace
  (ratings, rebooking), share engine everywhere.
- ✅ *Gate:* the scene self-sustains when you stop pushing.

### STAGE 3 — Monetize the Moat & Expand · Month 6–12 · 10k→100k
- ON: **B2B paid tier = the OS (plan + staff + run + verified-attendance data)**,
  consumer premium (RevenueCat, cosmetics), fan-led pull campaigns at scale,
  notification mesh matured, **second scene** (only when scene #1 runs itself).
- ✅ *Gate:* paying hosts in scene #1 before splitting focus.

### STAGE 4 — Platform · Month 12+ · 100k→1M+
- ON: gifting/creator economy (regulated fintech — LAST), gig payments/escrow,
  multi-city, partnerships/API, accountability layer (only now does community
  judgment carry weight — earned the right to the stick by proving the carrot).

## 3. The retention spine (the 5 days you're not out)
Reels/stories (filler) · Thursday planning pull · streak & rank upkeep ·
memory resurfacing ("1 year ago you Touched Down at…") · crew DMs ·
notification mesh ("your crew is out", "you're on the guest list", "your
request got played 🎧").

## 4. Metrics per stage
- S0: loop completion rate on device.
- S1: Weekly non-founder Touch Downs; week-2 retention.
- S2: venue-session length (time in-app DURING an event you Touched Down at —
  the real 3-hr metric); Crossed-Paths density; D30.
- S3: paying hosts; ARPU; scene-2 liquidity.
- Never: raw DAU, scroll minutes.

## 5. Discipline (what kills this)
1. Building instead of distributing (the #1 killer — founder discipline).
2. Turning features on before their density gate.
3. Web-only starving the moat (Touch Down needs native GPS + push).
4. Letting reels become the soul (we'd become the thing we're disrupting).
5. Public shaming scores (lawsuit + mission betrayal — positive-only reputation).
6. Handling money before Stage 4 (broker recognition, not transactions).
