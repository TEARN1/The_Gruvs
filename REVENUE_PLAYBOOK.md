# The Gruvs — Revenue Playbook (250+ ways to make money)

> Principles:
> 1. **Don't interrupt the vibe.** Ads/offers are *native* — they live inside the
>    feed, map, check-in, and after-movie, not as pop-ups over the fun.
> 2. **Businesses pay; users enjoy (and some earn).**
> 3. **You earn without handling money** where possible — Apple/Google/brands/partners
>    do the money part.
>
> Legend: **✅** solo-safe (someone else handles the money) · **💳** needs a payment
> rail/PSP (do at scale, with a provider) · **👤** = a way *users* make money.

## How to read this (so it's actionable, not overwhelming)
- **~230 are ✅ solo-safe** — they make money through ads, subscriptions, boosts,
  affiliate, B2B, and licensing, where Apple/Google/brands/partners handle the cash.
- **~10 are 💳** — gifting, in-app ticketing, marketplace commission, creator
  payouts. These need a payment provider + compliance → **scale-stage, not now.**
- **40 are 👤 user-earning** — your creator/hustle economy. Most are solo-safe if the
  **brand pays the user directly** or earnings are **vouchers/airtime/XP**; true cash
  payouts to users need a payouts provider (later).
